🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
This week the question takes an odd turn: Odysseus finally reaches Ithaca, the home he has spent six books trying to reach, and he cannot show his face there yet. Before he can ask anything of his community, he has to see what it has become while wearing another man’s skin. Watch what disguise reveals about the people he meets, and what it costs him to keep hiding who he is even from those he loves most.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — YOUR OWN COPY (FAGLES)
Books 13–16
Scene anchors to watch for: the Phaeacians carry the sleeping Odysseus home to Ithaca and sail away; Athena disguises him as an old beggar; Odysseus takes shelter with the loyal swineherd Eumaeus; Telemachus sails home, evading the suitors’ ambush; father and son are reunited at Eumaeus’s hut, and together they begin to plan against the suitors. This is a four-book stretch instead of the usual two, so pace yourself; see the “How to Read the Odyssey” guide for the week-by-week plan.
📋 WHAT’S IN THIS WEEK
Lesson
7.01 · Home in Disguise
Books 13–14: Odysseus arrives home disguised as a beggar and finds shelter with Eumaeus, a host who treats a stranger better than the suitors treat their king. Disguise and loyalty.
Lesson
7.02 · Father and Son
Books 15–16: Telemachus evades an ambush and comes home to Eumaeus’s hut, where Odysseus reveals himself. Recognition and dramatic irony, the reader knowing what the suitors do not.
Lesson
7.03 · What the Reader Knows
A writing-technique lesson: how to build a scene around the gap between what a reader knows and what a character does not, building toward Quick Write 7.
Grammar & Word Study
7.GV · Pronoun Clarity
Keeping pronouns clear and in agreement with what they refer to, plus this week’s Word Study words from Books 13–16.
Composition
Quick Write 7 · What the Reader Knows
Revise a scene from your Bard’s Hall tale so the reader knows something a character does not yet. A feeder toward your module project.
VR
VR Activity 7.01 · The Stranger at the Door
A solo activity staging a moment of disguise and recognition, the way Odysseus arrives at Eumaeus’s hut unknown.
VR
VR Activity 7.02 · What the Room Doesn’t Know
A solo activity building a scene where one detail in a space carries hidden meaning that only the audience is told.
Recitation

💪 BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Open your Module 1 Organizer and keep it nearby all week. With four books to track this week, jot down each new character Odysseus meets while disguised, and note who treats the stranger well before finding out who he is. Start with Lesson 7.01.
