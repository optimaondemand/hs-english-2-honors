🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
Last week you watched Demodocus the bard sing of Troy while Odysseus wept in silence, still hidden. This week the narrator steps aside and Odysseus takes over his own story. Watch what he chooses to tell, and how he tells it: the same guest-and-host pattern you traced at Pylos and Sparta reappears here in monstrous, inverted form. A host who devours his guests instead of feeding them breaks the rule the poem has been building all along.
