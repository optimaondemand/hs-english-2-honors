🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
Last week you saw that rule broken: a household of guests who took without asking and refused to leave. This week Telemachus travels to two other households, ruled by Nestor and by Menelaus, and finds the rule kept exactly as it should be. Watch what a household looks like when its king still knows how to receive a stranger.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — YOUR OWN COPY (FAGLES)
Books 3–4
Scene anchors to watch for: Telemachus arriving at Pylos and meeting Nestor; the sacrifice on the shore and the feast that follows; the journey on to Sparta; Menelaus and Helen’s court; the story of Troy remembered with grief; the news, learned from the sea-god Proteus, that Odysseus is alive but held on Calypso’s island.
