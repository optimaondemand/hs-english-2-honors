🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
Last week Odysseus outwitted a monster and steered his men past two great dangers with cunning alone. This week the stakes rise higher than they have yet gone. He travels beyond the edge of the living world to buy the knowledge he needs to get home, and then, in Book 12, loses his entire crew, not to strength or bad luck, but to a failure of restraint. Watch closely what each loss costs, and who pays for whose choice.
