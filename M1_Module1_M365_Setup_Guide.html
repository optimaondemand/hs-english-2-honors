M365 Setup & File Organization Guide
Everything you need to open, name, save, and submit your Word documents this year
📖 WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS
Almost everything you turn in this year in English 2, from your Quick Writes to your final projects, is a Word document that you create, name, save, and submit through Microsoft 365. Every assignment this module will link back to this page with a short reminder, so you never have to guess at the steps. Read it once now, carefully, and you will barely have to think about it again all year.
📚 PART 1 · OPEN WORD FROM YOUR SCHOOL ACCOUNT

You have a Microsoft 365 account through Optima Academy Online, and it is different from any personal Microsoft or Outlook account you might use at home. Your school account is the one tied to your Optima email address, and it is the only account you should use for coursework. Using your personal account by mistake is the single most common mix-up, so check this every time you sit down to write.

1
Go to office.com in your browser (not a personal Microsoft account page, and not an app store search).
2
Sign in with your Optima school email address and password. If you are already signed in on a school device, you may land here automatically.
3
Click Word from the app grid and choose New blank document. This opens Word in your browser, saved automatically to your school OneDrive as you type. (The desktop Word app works too, as long as it is signed in with your school account.)
💡 WHY IT MATTERS
A document created in a personal account lives in a different OneDrive than the one your teacher can see. It can look finished to you and still never reach your teacher. Working from your school account the whole time is what keeps your work visible, backed up, and easy to submit.
📚 PART 2 · NAMING YOUR FILE

Every file you submit this year follows one naming rule. It looks fussy the first time and becomes automatic by the second. The rule exists so that your teacher, looking through dozens of files from dozens of students, can tell instantly whose work it is and what it is.

📝 THE NAMING RULE, ANNOTATED
Smith_Jane_BardsHall_ELA10_Draft1.docx
LASTNAME_FIRSTNAME
Your legal name as it appears in Canvas. Spelled the same way every time you submit.
ASSIGNMENTSHORTNAME
A short label for the assignment, no spaces (for example QuickWrite1 or BardsHall). Use the short name given on the assignment page.
ELA10
The course code. Every English 2 file ends with ELA10, no matter the assignment.
DRAFT MARKER (WHEN NEEDED)
_Draft1, _Draft2, or _Final, added only for multi-draft work like a module project. A one-time Quick Write does not need a draft marker.

Most Quick Writes are a single submission, so their file name simply ends after the course code: Smith_Jane_QuickWrite1_ELA10.docx. Longer, multi-part work such as your module project (The Bard’s Hall) moves through stages, so its file name changes as the work grows:

▶
Smith_Jane_BardsHall_ELA10_Draft1.docx — your first full draft
▶
Smith_Jane_BardsHall_ELA10_Draft2.docx — your revised draft, after feedback
▶
Smith_Jane_BardsHall_ELA10_Final.docx — the version you submit for a grade
📚 PART 3 · WHERE YOUR FILES LIVE IN ONEDRIVE

Keep every English 2 file in one place so you can always find it again, and so anything shared with a teacher points to the right folder. Build this simple path in your OneDrive once, at the start of the year, and save everything for this course into it:

Optima → ELA → ELA10

If you are enrolled in more than one Optima course, this same Optima folder can hold a matching subfolder for each one (for example, an ELA folder for English and a separate folder for another subject). Everything for English 2 this year, every Quick Write, your Module 1 Organizer, and your project drafts, belongs inside Optima → ELA → ELA10.

📚 PART 4 · SUBMITTING THROUGH CANVAS

You do not attach files from your computer to submit work in this course. Instead, each assignment page has a OneDrive tab built directly into Canvas, and you submit straight from your OneDrive through it. This keeps one single copy of your file as the version of record, instead of scattering copies across your device and Canvas.

1
Open the assignment in Canvas and scroll to Submit Assignment.
2
Choose the Microsoft OneDrive tab (sometimes shown as a OneDrive icon) rather than File Upload.
3
Browse to Optima → ELA → ELA10 and select your correctly named file.
4
Confirm the submission and check that Canvas shows a green submitted confirmation before you close the tab.

💪 BEFORE YOU GO

Four habits carry you through the whole year: sign in with your school account, name every file the same way, keep everything in one OneDrive folder, and submit through the OneDrive tab. None of it is complicated once you have done it once. Come back to this page any time an assignment’s M365 reminder points you here, and you will always find the full steps waiting.
