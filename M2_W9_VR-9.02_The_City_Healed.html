Solo VR Activity · Week 9 · Completion-graded
Note for build planning: this page describes what you will do and why. Step-by-step directions for your specific device or platform will be added later. For now, focus on the goal below.
🎯 OBJECTIVE
Back in Week 1, you staged the suppliants gathered at the palace doors, begging their king to save a city dying of plague. The play itself never shows you Thebes healed; it ends with a blinded king going into exile and a chorus warning the crowd. In this closing activity, working solo, you imagine forward past the play’s final scene: what does that same city look like on the day the plague actually lifts? You will stage the restored space and record a short reflection answering this module’s question in your own words.
🗺️ WHAT YOU’LL DO
1
Return to the same public space you staged in Week 1’s Altar at the Palace Doors activity, or arrange a new civic space of your choosing: a courtyard, a set of steps, a gathering place.
2
Clear away anything that represented the suppliants’ branches or signs of plague from Week 1, and arrange the space instead to show a city that can finally breathe: open gates, a restored altar, some sign of ordinary life resuming.
3
Standing in this restored space, record a short reflection, in your own words, answering the module’s question: what does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community, when the king himself is the source of the sickness?
4
Include one specific line of evidence from the play in your reflection, and say briefly why that moment shapes your own answer.
5
Save your capture and submit it, following the submission instructions below.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
A short (about 60 to 90 second) recording or capture of your restored civic space and your spoken reflection, in whatever device-agnostic form you have available: a video or screen recording with narration, an audio recording paired with a few photos or sketches of the staged space, or a short written reflection with a brief description if recording is not possible. Save it in your ELA10 OneDrive folder and submit through Canvas.
✅ COMPLETION CRITERIA
☐
The staged space visibly shows restoration: a change from Week 1’s plague imagery, not a repeat of it.
☐
The recorded reflection directly answers the module’s essential question, in the student’s own words.
☐
One specific piece of evidence from the play is named and briefly explained.
☐
The file is saved and submitted by the due date. (Completion credit; no rubric score.)
🏗️ FACILITATOR NOTE
This is a solo, asynchronous activity; there is no separate live version. A live teacher can support it by reminding students this is a personal reflection, not a performance; a brief, sincere answer is worth more than an elaborate staging. Common sticking point: students default to summarizing the play’s plot instead of actually answering the question in their own voice. Redirect them to state their own claim first, in a single sentence, before they explain the evidence behind it.
