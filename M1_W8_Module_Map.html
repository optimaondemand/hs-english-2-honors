🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
This week turns the question inside out. Odysseus stands in his own hall, and the community that owes him its loyalty does not even recognize him. Watch closely who treats a stranger with decency and who does not, since none of them yet know they are being watched by their own king.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — YOUR OWN COPY (FAGLES)
Books 17–20
Scene anchors to watch for: the disguised beggar entering his own hall; Antinous and the suitors’ abuse; the fight with the beggar Irus; the old dog Argos and his recognition; Odysseus’s conversation with Penelope; Eurycleia and the scar; Penelope’s decision to set the contest of the bow; the omens that gather before it.
