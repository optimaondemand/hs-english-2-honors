Solo VR Activity 4.01
Staging the Bard
Solo VR Activity · Week 4 · Completion-graded
Note for build planning: this page describes what you will do and why. Step-by-step directions for your specific device or platform will be added later. For now, focus on the goal below.
🎯 OBJECTIVE
Demodocus did not simply know a story about Troy; he performed it, in a real room, for a real king, in front of a guest whose reaction he could not have predicted. In this activity, working solo, you will stage that same moment, a bard called forward to perform before a court, so you can feel from the inside what it takes to hold a room with nothing but a voice and a story everyone half-knows. This is direct preparation for the telling you are building toward in your own Bard’s Hall.
🗺️ WHAT YOU’LL DO
1
Working solo, choose or arrange a space to represent a great hall: a room with a clear front and back, or a modern equivalent such as a small stage area or the head of a table.
2
Take the role of the bard. Stand or sit at the front of your space, the way Demodocus is given the place of honor at the feast, and announce the story you are about to tell (real or invented; it does not need to be finished yet).
3
Perform 30 to 60 seconds of the opening, using your voice deliberately: change your pace, pause before an important detail, and let your tone shift with the mood of the moment.
4
Narrate one sentence, out of character, about what changed in your own delivery once you thought of yourself as performing for a court rather than just reading words aloud.
5
Save your capture and submit it, following the submission instructions below.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
A short (about 60 to 90 second) recording or capture of your staged performance, in whatever device-agnostic form you have available: a video or screen recording with narration, an audio recording paired with a few photos or sketches, or a short written script with a brief description of your delivery choices if recording is not possible. Save it in your ELA10 OneDrive folder and submit through Canvas.
✅ COMPLETION CRITERIA
☐
The recording shows a clear performance of an opening, not a flat reading.
☐
At least one deliberate delivery choice (pace, pause, or tone shift) is noticeable.
☐
One sentence names what changed once the activity was framed as a performance.
☐
The file is saved and submitted by the due date. (Completion credit; no rubric score.)
🏗️ FACILITATOR NOTE
This is a solo, asynchronous activity; there is no separate live version. A live teacher can support it by modeling one 15-second delivery shift (flat reading, then the same line performed with pace and pause) before students try it themselves. Common sticking point: students read their opening in a flat, uniform tone; redirect them to vary pace and pause deliberately, since that is what the completion criteria actually require.
