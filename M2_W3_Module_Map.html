🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
When a community suffers, what does it owe its king, and what does the king owe it?
This week the question turns inward, toward the advocate’s task ahead of you. Oedipus accuses the one man defending himself with the clearest reasoning in the play so far. His wife tries to calm him with a story meant to prove prophecy false, and instead hands him the one detail that should terrify him. Watch closely how each person in this week’s reading builds, or fails to build, an honest case, because you are about to build one of your own.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — BY SCENE LANDMARK
The Creon quarrel through the summons of the herdsman
Scene landmarks to watch for: Creon’s self-defense against the charge of conspiracy; Oedipus’s refusal to hear him out; Jocasta stepping between the two men and telling the old story of Laius and the oracle; the phrase “three roads” catching in Oedipus’s throat; Oedipus telling his own story of Corinth, Delphi, and a killing at a crossroads; the sending for the one surviving witness, an old herdsman. Read the full text free at Project Gutenberg #31 if you want it alongside the passages quoted in your lessons.
📋 WHAT’S IN THIS WEEK
Lesson
3.01 · The Reasonable Man
Creon’s self-defense as a model argument, and why the accused, not the king, gives the most reasonable speech in the play so far.
Lesson
3.02 · Three Roads
Jocasta’s story of Laius and the oracle, Oedipus’s own Corinth story, and the two accounts converging in front of an audience that can already see what neither speaker can.
Lesson
3.03 · Say It Faithfully, Then Weigh It
A writing-technique lesson: restating a passage faithfully in your own words before you argue about what it proves, building toward Quick Write 11.

Using because, although, since, and if…then clauses to make the logic of an argument visible, plus this week’s Word Study words.
Composition

Paraphrase one key speech from this week faithfully, then weigh what it proves and does not prove toward a verdict. Feeder 3 toward the Trial of Oedipus.
