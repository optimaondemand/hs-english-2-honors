🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
Every text this year returns to some version of this question. This week, the Odyssey opens with the answer turned upside down: the king is gone, and the community meant to wait for him has instead begun to consume his household. Keep this question in view as you read Books 1 and 2.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — YOUR OWN COPY (FAGLES)
Books 1–2
Scene anchors to watch for: the gods’ council on Olympus; Athena’s visit to Telemachus in disguise; the suitors overrunning the house; Telemachus calling the assembly on Ithaca.
