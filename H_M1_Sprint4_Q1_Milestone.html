🎮 DESIGN FOCUS
Bringing Quarter 1 together into one milestone
The Designer’s Table · Q1 milestone: pitch + lore + prototype v0 + annotated sources
This is the sprint where everything you built this quarter comes together. First, decide what your game owes to the Odyssey itself: a genuine design idea worth using, or one worth deliberately setting aside. Then put your own prototype through an honest AI critique before you gather your pitch, your lore document, your prototype, and your sources into a single Q1 milestone.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Apply the Odyssey’s design lens to your own game: use one of its ideas about the rules of a world, a journey, or the “house rules” of hospitality, or deliberately reject it with reasons. Ask an AI tool to red-team your game for what is unclear or not fun, and respond to its critique in writing. Then record a short pitch and compile your full Q1 milestone: pitch, world/lore document, prototype v0, and your annotated sources so far.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Does your world run on rules like a host and a stranger negotiating trust, and if not, why not?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Apply the Odyssey design lens. Consider three ideas the epic offers a designer: the rules of a world (a society ordered by reputation, oaths, and the sacred bond between guest and host, rather than written law); a journey and its sequence of trials; or the “house rules” of hospitality (how a household tests and is tested by a stranger at the door). Decide whether your game will build one of these directly into its rules, or deliberately reject it. Write a short paragraph explaining your choice and your reasoning either way.
2
Get an AI red-team critique. Describe your rules and your prototype v0 to an AI tool and ask it directly: “What is unclear or not fun about this game so far?” Read its full critique.
3
Respond in writing. For each point the AI raised, write your own response: agree, and explain the fix you will make, or disagree, and explain in your own reasoning why the critique misses something true about your design.
4
Record your pitch. Record a short spoken pitch, sixty to ninety seconds, on any device, covering: your world, your design question, your core mechanic and win condition, and why the question matters.
5
Compile your Q1 milestone. Gather four pieces into one submission: your pitch recording, your world/lore document (Sprint 2), your prototype v0 description or photo (Sprint 3), and your annotated sources (every JStor find you have logged so far, with full citations).
6
Log your AI use. Add one complete row to your AI-Use Log for the red-team step: what you asked, what it gave you, and how you responded to each point.
⚠️ AI-USE REMINDER
An AI critique can flag confusing rules, but it cannot tell you whether your game is actually fun; only real playtesting, later in the year, answers that. Treat this red-team step as a rules-clarity check, not a verdict on the game itself, and keep your AI-Use Log honest about what you kept and what you rejected.

💡 ABOUT THIS MILESTONE
This is a Tier 1/2 completion milestone, like every sprint before it. Nothing here needs the finish or polish of a final product yet. The Designer’s Table carries a single graded Tier 3 defense, later, in Quarter 4, once the game itself has gone through real playtesting and revision.
