🎯 THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ASKS
What does a community owe its king, and what does a king owe his community?
This week the poem finally turns to the king himself. Odysseus is not part of any community right now. He is a castaway between two very different hosts: a goddess who would keep him forever on her island, and a foreign people who choose to help a stranger move on. Watch what each place asks of him, and what it offers, as you read Books 5 and 6.
📖 THIS WEEK’S READING — YOUR OWN COPY (FAGLES)
Books 5–6
Scene anchors to watch for: Odysseus freed from Calypso’s island; the raft he builds and the storm that wrecks it; washing ashore exhausted; Nausicaa and her maids finding him; the world of the Phaeacians.
