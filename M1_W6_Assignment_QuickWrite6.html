📖 WRITING FOCUS
Building to a turning point
Narrative craft · builds toward your Module 1 project, The Bard’s Hall
This week you watched a warning, given clearly in Book 11, get worn down by hunger and broken in Book 12, at the cost of an entire crew. In Lesson 6.03 you practiced the three moves behind a strong turning point: a clear cost, mounting pressure, and a choice that cannot be undone. Now you write that exact moment for your own Bard’s Hall tale.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Write the highest-stakes turning-point moment of your Bard’s Hall tale (200 to 300 words). Show a clear cost your character or their group already knows about, real pressure that makes it harder to honor, and the exact instant a choice becomes impossible to take back.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What is the one moment in your story where the most is at risk, and how do you make a reader feel that it cannot be undone?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Choose or continue the moment in your Bard’s Hall story where the stakes are highest: the point where your character stands to lose the most, whatever “the most” means in your tale.
2
Build the scene with the three moves from Lesson 6.03: name a clear cost your character already knows, apply real pressure that makes it harder to honor, and land on a choice that cannot be undone once it is made.
3
Hold one consistent verb tense across the whole scene, and use active voice deliberately at the exact moment the choice is made, so the sentence puts the responsibility where it belongs.
4
Work in at least two of this week’s Word Study words (seer, phantom, forewarn, famine, prodigious, invincible), used accurately. Reread once and ask: does this moment feel like it changes what is possible in the rest of the story?
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
The scene names a clear cost, shows mounting pressure, and lands on a choice that cannot be undone.
☐
Verb tense is consistent throughout the scene.
☐
Active voice is used deliberately at the moment of highest stakes.
☐
At least two of this week’s Word Study words are used accurately.
☐
The writing is 200 to 300 words.
📊 HOW IT’S SCORED
Criteria Full credit Partial Pts
Turning point built
5 pts		 A clear cost, mounting pressure, and an irreversible choice are all present and land convincingly. One of the three moves is missing or unclear. 5
Tense & voice
3 pts		 Tense is consistent throughout; active voice is used deliberately at the key moment. A tense shift appears, or the key moment is written in passive voice. 3
Word Study & length
2 pts		 At least two Word Study words used accurately; 200 to 300 words. Fewer than two Word Study words, or off the length target. 2
Total: 10 pts
