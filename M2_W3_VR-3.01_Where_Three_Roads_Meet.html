Solo VR Activity 3.01
Where Three Roads Meet
Solo VR Activity · Week 3 · Completion-graded
Note for build planning: this page describes what you will do and why. Step-by-step directions for your specific device or platform will be added later. For now, focus on the goal below.
🎯 OBJECTIVE
A single, ordinary place, where three roads meet, turns out to hold the whole truth of this play. In this activity, working solo, you will stage that crossroads and think through it the way an investigator would: not as a dramatic symbol, but as an actual scene with actual physical facts. This sharpens the same close attention to physical evidence you will need when you build your own case in the Trial of Oedipus.
🗺️ WHAT YOU’LL DO
1
Working solo, choose or arrange a space to represent a crossroads where three paths converge: an intersection, a place where three hallways or paths meet, or a simple sketch or model of one if no real space is available.
2
Stand at the center point where the three paths meet. Look down each of the three roads in turn, and narrate out loud, for each one, what an investigator would want to know: who travels this way, how wide is it, what could a person coming from this direction not have seen coming from another.
3
Narrate what a careful investigator would ask about this exact spot: how many people would it take to overpower a traveler here, could a witness at this point have seen the whole encounter or only part of it, and what would make one person’s account of “robbers” more or less believable given the layout.
4
Close by stating, in one or two sentences, one question this physical walk-through raises that the play itself never directly answers.
5
Save your capture and submit it, following the submission instructions below.
📤 WHAT TO SUBMIT
A short (about 60 to 90 second) recording or capture of your investigator’s walk-through, in whatever device-agnostic form you have available: a screen or video recording with narration, an audio recording paired with a few photos or sketches of your three-road setup, or a short written script with a brief description if recording is not possible. Save it in your ELA10 OneDrive folder and submit through Canvas.
✅ COMPLETION CRITERIA
☐
All three roads are addressed individually, not treated as one generic direction.
☐
At least one specific investigator’s question is raised about the physical scene itself (line of sight, number of attackers, layout).
☐
One closing question the play does not directly answer is stated clearly.
☐
The file is saved and submitted by the due date. (Completion credit; no rubric score.)
🏗️ FACILITATOR NOTE
This is a solo, asynchronous activity; there is no separate live version. A live teacher can support it by modeling one road’s worth of investigator questions (30 seconds) before students try all three on their own. Common sticking point: students describe the crossroads emotionally (“this is where everything went wrong”) instead of physically (sightlines, width, number of people it would take); redirect them to the completion criteria, which requires specific physical questions about the scene.
